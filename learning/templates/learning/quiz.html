The user pushed back: the hero felt school-report-card-y, "owned" was odd phrasing, the chip wall was padding, and the score was buried in a tiny footer. v2 principles: • The hero is the ACTION ("Drill the 5 you missed"), not a metric. That's what the learner needs to do next; everything else is context. Inverting "stat → button" hierarchy puts the next step where the eye lands. • Score IS visible — as a small celebration pill above the action, not a banner. Honest about gamification, not the centerpiece. • Stats compressed to a single horizontal microline (right · pct · rating delta). One row, not three sections. • No chip wall of correct words. The wins are implicit in the "9 right" count and the absence from the missed list. • Teaching cards retained — meaning + translation, no wrong-answer exposure. Sit below the action so they don't compete for attention. {% endcomment %} {% comment %} Continuity-layer "teacher voice" header — appears ABOVE the rank stamp.
